§1 Introduction

SMM Chat ("the Service", "we", "us") is a messaging tool that lets a business connect its WhatsApp Business Account and Instagram Professional account to a single inbox, so a team can view and reply to customer conversations from one place. This policy explains what data the Service collects when a business connects those accounts, why, and how it is handled.

This is a development-stage policy published to satisfy Meta's platform requirements while the app is being built and tested. It should be reviewed and replaced with counsel-approved terms before the Service is used with real customer data in production.

§2 Information we collect

The Service collects data at two points: when a business connects an account, and when messages flow through it afterward.

§3 Data from Meta platforms

The Service uses the Meta Graph API to send and receive WhatsApp and Instagram messages on a connected business's behalf. This requires permissions such as whatsapp_business_messaging, instagram_basic, and instagram_manage_messages. Data obtained through these permissions is used only to operate the messaging inbox described in this policy — not for advertising, and not shared with any data broker.

Use of information received from Meta APIs is subject to and limited by the Meta Developer Platform Terms and Meta's Data Policy, in addition to this policy.

§4 How we use information

  • Display conversations and message history to the business's authorized team members.
  • Send outbound messages, media, and read receipts through the Graph API on the business's behalf.
  • Route inbound webhook events from Meta to the correct connected business account.
  • Keep the connection to Meta active, including refreshing access tokens.
  • Diagnose and fix delivery failures (for example, a message that Meta rejected).

We do not use conversation content to train models, and we do not sell contact data.

§5 How information is shared

Data is shared only as needed to operate the Service:

  • With Meta — every outbound message is transmitted through Meta's Graph API, which is how WhatsApp and Instagram delivery works.
  • Within the business — team members added to a business account can see that business's conversations, scoped by their role.
  • With infrastructure providers — hosting and database providers that store the Service's data, bound by standard confidentiality obligations.

We do not share data across unrelated businesses using the Service — each business's conversations are isolated from every other business's.

§6 Retention

Conversation history is retained for as long as a business keeps its account connected, so past messages remain visible in the inbox. A business can disconnect a WhatsApp or Instagram account at any time; doing so stops new messages from syncing but keeps existing history unless the business separately requests deletion.

§7 Security

Access tokens and conversation data are stored in the Service's database and used only by the Service's own servers to call Meta's API — they are never exposed to a customer's device. Access to a business's data within the Service requires an authenticated, role-scoped account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

§8 Your choices & rights

A connected business can disconnect its WhatsApp or Instagram account at any time, which stops the Service from sending or receiving further messages for it. A business's customers who message a connected account can also stop messaging it, or block it, using WhatsApp's or Instagram's own controls — the Service does not override those platform-level choices.

To request a copy or deletion of data the Service holds about a specific business or contact, use the contact details in §11.

§9 Children's privacy

The Service is intended for use by businesses and their staff, not by children. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children through the Service.

§10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. The "Effective" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
