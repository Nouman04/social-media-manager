SMM Chat

How to Delete Your Data

Effective September 6, 2026 Version 1.0

§1 Who this applies to

SMM Chat connects a business's WhatsApp Business Account and/or Instagram Professional account so its team can reply to customer messages from one inbox. This page explains how to request deletion of data SMM Chat holds — whether you're a connected business, one of its team members, or a customer who has messaged a business using SMM Chat.

§2 If you're a connected business

Disconnecting an account stops new messages from syncing immediately:

  1. Open the account's connect page (/api/v1/instagram/connect or the WhatsApp equivalent) and sign in.
  2. Select the business, then remove the connected account.

To also delete the conversation history, account credentials, and business records SMM Chat stored, email us using the contact in §5 — include your business name and the connected WhatsApp number / Instagram username so we can locate the right records.

§3 If you're a customer who messaged a business

SMM Chat only stores messages you sent to a business that uses it — it has no way to look you up otherwise. To request deletion of your messages:

  • Ask the business you messaged to remove your conversation, or
  • Email us at the contact in §5 with the business's name/username and the WhatsApp number or Instagram username you messaged from, so we can locate and delete the matching conversation.
Blocking or deleting the conversation from within WhatsApp or Instagram stops you from being messaged again, but doesn't by itself delete what's already stored on our side — use the request above for that.

§4 What gets deleted

A deletion request removes the matching conversation's message history, contact identifiers (phone number or Instagram-scoped ID), and any locally cached media from our database. Where we're required to keep a record for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons, we'll tell you what's retained and why when we respond.

We aim to complete a verified deletion request within 30 days.

§5 Contact

Send data deletion requests to privacy@your-domain.example — replace with your real support address before sharing this page outside of testing. Include enough detail (business name, phone number, or Instagram username) for us to identify the right records; we may ask you to verify you're the account holder before deleting anything.
